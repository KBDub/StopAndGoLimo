## H2: The History of House of Blues Chicago

### H3: A Marina City Landmark Since 1996

House of Blues Chicago opened in 1996 inside the historic Marina City complex along the Chicago River in River North, co-founded in part by Blues Brothers star Dan Aykroyd. The venue's interior was designed to evoke a Southern Delta juke joint layered with the grandeur of a European opera house, reportedly inspired by Prague's Estates Theatre, creating one of the more visually distinctive concert spaces in the city.

### H3: A Stage for Blues Legends and Beyond

Over its decades downtown, House of Blues Chicago has hosted an enormous range of performers, from blues and soul legends to major touring acts across rock, hip-hop, and pop, all within a 1,400-capacity room known for its intimate, tiered layout that puts fans close to the stage.

### H3: The World Famous Gospel Brunch Tradition

One of House of Blues Chicago's most beloved institutions is its Sunday Gospel Brunch, pairing an all-you-can-eat Southern-style buffet with live gospel performances — a tradition that combines soul food and soul music into one of the most unique recurring events in Chicago nightlife.

### H4: Why This History Shapes Your Evening

House of Blues Chicago blends musical legacy, only-in-Chicago architecture, and Southern hospitality in a way few venues in the city can match, and our chauffeurs bring the same appreciation for that character to every River North drop-off.
