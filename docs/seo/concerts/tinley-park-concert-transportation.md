## H2: The History of Tinley Park's Amphitheater

### H3: From World Music Theatre to Credit Union 1 Amphitheatre

The venue opened in 1990 as the World Music Theatre, one of the first purpose-built outdoor amphitheaters in the Chicago area, with a capacity built to rival any venue in the region — roughly 11,000 reserved seats and another 17,000 on the lawn. Over more than three decades, the venue has carried six different names as sponsorships changed hands: World Music Theatre, New World Music Theatre, Tweeter Center, First Midwest Bank Amphitheatre, Hollywood Casino Amphitheatre, and, since 2023, Credit Union 1 Amphitheatre.

### H3: A Launching Pad for Legendary Chicagoland Concert Nights

Cher performed the venue's opening night in 1990, and in the decades since, the amphitheater has hosted an enormous range of legendary acts and moments — including an early-career Lollapalooza stop in 1993 featuring Alice in Chains, Primus, and Rage Against the Machine, and performances from superstar-level touring artists across every genre.

### H3: One of the Region's True Mega-Venues

With a capacity that makes it one of the two largest amphitheaters in the greater Chicago area, Tinley Park's venue continues to be the go-to stage for summer tour stops that need serious scale — meaning big crowds, big parking lots, and a real need for group transportation that skips the chaos entirely.

### H4: Why the Name Changes Don't Matter to Us

Regardless of what the marquee says this season, our chauffeurs know this venue's access roads, gate logistics, and exit patterns inside and out — because the traffic challenge has stayed exactly the same across all six names.
